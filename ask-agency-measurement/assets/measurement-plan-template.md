# Agency Measurement Plan — [Tool / Program name]

> A working plan, structured as the key questions to answer in each section. Answer them in plain language. Where an answer needs a specific framework, instrument, or method this guide doesn't contain, note it as "to research" and use the research queries. Delete the worked example once your own content is in.

## 1. Context
- What is the tool, and what does it do for the user?
- Who uses it — where, in what language, under what conditions and constraints?
- What is the user's **end goal** the tool is meant to support?
- What **decision** will this measurement inform — iterating the product, or judging impact?
- Where does agency sit in your **theory of change**, in one sentence?

## 2. Definition of agency for this problem
- In one or two plain sentences, what does "agency" mean for *these* users and *this* goal?
- Which **form(s)** are in scope: agency toward the end goal, agency in relation to the tool, or both?
- Which **manifestation(s)**: experienced (felt internally), expressed (shown externally), or both?
- Which **framework(s)** best fit, and why? What did your research surface that the starting examples missed?
- What would it look like for the tool to **undermine** agency here, not just fail to help?

## 4. Measurement approaches (per cell in scope)
- For each cell: which **tool type**, on or off platform, at what **moment**, and the single biggest **bias** to guard against?
- For each: is there an existing **validated instrument** to adapt, or do we need to build one (and research which)?

## 5. Validation plan
- Which **validity/reliability** properties matter most for the claim you want to make, and why?
- What is the **cheapest credible** validation step you can run next?
- Are you comparing groups or cultures? If so, what is your plan to test **measurement invariance** before comparing scores?
- For any **LLM/naturalistic** measure: what is the gold standard, who builds it, and what is the triangulation plan? (Mark as experimental.)

## 6. What's left to research
- Which framework, instrument, or validation method do you still need to find?
- What are the exact **research queries** you will run, and in which tool?

## 7. Iterate
- What will you revisit as the program evolves and evidence accumulates?
- What is the next decision this plan should be updated to inform?
